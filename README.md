## Key Features

### User Input for Subjects
Users can input the name, difficulty (on a scale of 1 to 5), and deadlines for multiple subjects. Input validation ensures that difficulty levels are within the specified range.

### Subject Prioritization
Subjects are prioritized based on their difficulty using a greedy algorithm, ensuring that more challenging subjects receive appropriate attention.

### Study Hours Allocation
The program allocates study hours and minutes to subjects based on their difficulty and ensures that the total allocated time does not exceed available study hours. Adjustments are made to allocate extra time to subjects with upcoming exams.

### Optimization Algorithms
- **Hungarian Algorithm**: Used for optimizing the assignment of subjects to study tasks based on a cost matrix that factors in difficulty and deadlines.
- **Knapsack Algorithm**: Implements a classic dynamic programming approach to maximize the difficulty achieved with available study hours.

### Study Plan Generation
The project generates a daily study plan, displaying allocated hours and minutes for each subject based on their upcoming exam days. The plan is dynamically adjusted, ensuring that subjects with imminent exams receive extra study time.

### User-Friendly Menu Interface
An interactive menu allows users to easily navigate through options to input subjects, optimize their study plan, and view daily allocations.
